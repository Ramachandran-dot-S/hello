# HEALTHBRIDGE
A Python-powered UI app that helps communities track habits, wellness, and shared goals.
## How to use:
### 1. If you do not have an account or want to create another one, click on "create account".
<img width="922" height="557" alt="Login page" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/36a26278-42b3-47dc-be9b-e101089b69ae" />

### 2. To create an account, you will have to first enter all the details, and then click on "register profile".
<img width="918" height="558" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d78ccb73-ba84-4d75-90be-a8f2effc72c0" />

### 3. This takes you back to the first page where as you now have an account, you will have to enter the username and password that you created in the last step. Then, click on "sign in".
<img width="922" height="557" alt="Login page" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/704dc942-4615-4ada-ab3f-72e5d923306f" />

### 4. This will now take you to the dashboard of the account you logged into, showing stats, a score, focus areas, and an AI-generated lifestyle correlation.
<img width="1365" height="697" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f67b93ba-04e5-4e12-882b-449076f47f92" />

### 5. You can log your record by either clicking on the "Log Today's Record" in the dashboard or also by going to the "Log Daily Biometrics" tab and entering the data. Then, click on "commit and save record".
<img width="1365" height="694" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c902d2de-8ef1-4fdf-9d8c-1e107dde4e33" />

### 6. This changes the info on the dashboard and the AI-generated correlator starts giving you suggestions on how to improve your lifestyle.
<img width="1919" height="990" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/79c3f4a3-3900-400a-967a-1d5c9092b290" />

### 7. After some use, the AI Weekly Report starts to fill up and the AI lifestyle suggester starts giving more accurate ways to stay healthy and fit. Also, the trend direction starts changing.
<img width="1919" height="989" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/36a6e28d-e2ec-4fc4-9425-c224563440a2" />

### 8. The Biometric Trend Visualizer consists of four graphs that display the varying trends of sleep, hydration, physical activity, and mindfulness. These trends keep changing with every log you register.
<img width="1365" height="696" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/18767c83-a2c8-4d6d-abd6-1cd3ba469de1" />

### 9. The Data Ledger or the Historical Record Ledger allows you to search, modify, or export records.
<img width="1365" height="695" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/573ebc9f-bd00-4afa-838f-610e46392bd7" />

### 10. In HealthBridge, you can also change the daily benchmark targets and by how much each category changes the main percentage you see in the dashboard. This can be done by going to the "Weights and Targets" tab.
<img width="1365" height="695" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7881c240-7b58-4284-9008-6e978ac81878" />

### 11. There are also two demos in HealthBridge.
#### a. The balanced scenario shows an account that is nearly perfect in its different parameters.
<img width="1365" height="696" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/621e30d4-4374-4582-92ce-5f3d9621f7ec" />

#### b. The burnout scenario shows an account that seriously requires attention in all the various parameters.
<img width="1365" height="696" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c8baf82a-8cca-4c1a-9f1e-6707426d32eb" />

### 12. Lastly, there is also a function in which you can sign out of your account and it will then take you to the sign in page fro the beginning.

## How it is useful:
### * It helps to monitor mental well-being.
### * It helps in tracking fitness, sleep, hydration and Mindfulness.
### * It keeps you aware about your daily habits.
### * It gives you AI lifestyle suggestions.

### * SDG 3 - Good health and well-being.
### * SDG 10 - Reduced inequalities.
